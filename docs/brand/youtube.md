never quote the internal emotional promise as a slogan. Store terminology comes

[`lib/brand.ts`](../../apps/extension/lib/brand.ts).

## Per-video metadata template

- **Title** (at most 70 characters, hook first):
  `<Hook: the outcome or question> | Snug` Example:
  `Export one bookmarks folder as HTML, JSON or CSV | Snug`
- **Description:**
  1. Line 1:
     `Get Snug: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/gdhpeilfkeeajillmcncaelnppiakjhn`
  2. One or two sentences on what the video shows, in plain words.
  3. Timestamps, only when the video is longer than 60 seconds (first one at
     `0:00`).
  4. A local, on-device reminder: no account, nothing uploaded.
  5. At most 3 hashtags, e.g. `#bookmarks #chromeextension #snug`.
- **Tags:**
  `snug, bookmarks, export bookmarks, import bookmarks, backup bookmarks, chrome extension`,
  plus one or two specific to the video.
- **Category:** Science & Technology
- **Language:** English (set the title and description language per upload)
- **Upload defaults:**
  - Visibility: Public (Unlisted for review drafts).
  - Audience: not made for kids.
  - Paid promotion: no.
  - Altered content: no.
  - Comments: on, basic moderation.
  - License: Standard YouTube License.
  - Add to the "Getting started" playlist when it fits.
